# Networking Portfolio

A collection of hands-on networking labs and troubleshooting projects documenting my transition into networking after earning my CCNA.

Hi, I'm Yaro. I recently transitioned into networking and earned my CCNA. My background is in professional photography and 3D printing, and I’m now building hands-on networking labs and documentation to demonstrate practical networking skills.

---

## About Me

After earning my CCNA, I began building labs focused on routing, switching, and troubleshooting while also starting to learn foundational network automation.

My goal is to move into an entry-level networking or network support role and continue developing practical skills in routing, switching, troubleshooting, and automation.

My previous work in photography and 3D printing helped me develop attention to detail, documentation habits, troubleshooting discipline, and client-focused problem solving, all of which I now apply to networking projects.

## Projects

### OSPF Golden Configuration and Troubleshooting Lab

Three-router single-area OSPF lab documenting a verified baseline, neighbor formation, route learning, LSDB validation, and a structured troubleshooting scenario.

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/yaro-var/ospf-troubleshooting-lab

---

### VLAN DHCP Troubleshooting Lab

Cisco switching lab documenting VLANs, trunking, router-on-a-stick, DHCP, and a completed troubleshooting scenario where PC1 fails to obtain a DHCP lease due to a missing VLAN configuration.

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/yaro-var/vlan-troubleshooting-lab

---

### SSH Compatibility Troubleshooting Lab

Troubleshooting an SSH connection failure between a modern Windows OpenSSH client and a legacy Cisco router due to cryptographic algorithm mismatches. Demonstrates step-by-step resolution of key exchange, host key, and MAC negotiation failures.

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/yaro-var/ssh-compatibility-troubleshooting-lab
