# Networking Portfolio

A collection of hands-on networking labs and troubleshooting projects documenting my transition into networking after earning my CCNA.

Hi, I'm Yaro. I recently transitioned into networking and earned my CCNA. My background is in professional photography and 3D printing, and I’m now building hands-on networking labs and documentation to demonstrate practical networking skills.

## About Me

After earning my CCNA, I began building labs focused on routing, switching, and troubleshooting while also starting to learn foundational network automation. My goal is to move into an entry-level networking or network support role and continue developing practical skills in routing, switching, troubleshooting, and automation.

My previous work in photography and 3D printing helped me develop attention to detail, documentation habits, troubleshooting discipline, and client-focused problem solving, all of which I now apply to networking projects.

## Projects

### OSPF Golden Configuration and Troubleshooting Lab

Three-router single-area OSPF lab documenting a verified golden baseline, neighbor formation, route learning, LSDB validation, and a documented troubleshooting scenario.

**Repository:** [ospf-troubleshooting-lab](https://github.com/yaro-var/ospf-troubleshooting-lab)

### VLAN DHCP Troubleshooting Lab

Cisco CML switching lab documenting VLANs, trunking, router-on-a-stick, DHCP, and a completed troubleshooting scenario where PC1 fails to obtain a DHCP lease because VLAN 10 is missing from SW2.

**Repository:** [vlan-troubleshooting-lab](https://github.com/yaro-var/vlan-troubleshooting-lab)

### Small Office Network Design

Small branch office network design including segmentation, IP addressing, and supporting documentation.

**Status:** In progress
